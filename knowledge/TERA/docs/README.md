## Quick Reference

### TERA Eligibility
- 15-20 years of active service
- Service Secretary approval required
- Voluntary application
- Program must be active for your branch

### Retirement Formula
```
Monthly Pay = Years of Service × 0.025 × High-36 Average Pay
```

### Example
- 17 years service
- $6,000 high-36 average
- Calculation: 17 × 0.025 × $6,000 = $2,550/month
