# VA Legal Resources

Information about legal help for VA claims.

## Free Legal Help

### Veterans Service Officers (VSOs)
- **Cost**: Free
- **Provided by**: VA, state, and veteran organizations
- **Services**: Represent you at VA, help file appeals
- **Accreditation**: Accredited by VA
- **Find**: https://www.va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation/

### Veterans Organizations
- **American Legion**: Free representation, nationwide presence
- **VFW** (Veterans of Foreign Wars): Free representation
- **DAV** (Disabled American Veterans): Free representation
- **AMVETS**: Free representation
- **Many others**: Search for local VSO

## Paid Legal Help

### VA-Accredited Attorneys
- **Cost**: May charge fee, can't exceed 20% of backpay
- **Services**: Full legal representation
- **Expertise**: VA law specialists
- **Find**: https://www.va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation/

### When to Consider Attorney
- Complex legal issues
- CAVC (court) appeal needed
- Denied multiple times
- Significant backpay amount

## CAVC Appeals (Court Level)

### Veterans Court of Appeals
- **Full Name**: Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims
- **Location**: Washington, D.C.
- **Requirement**: Lost Board of Veterans' Appeals case
- **Timeline**: File within 120 days of Board decision
- **Website**: https://www.uscourts.cavc.gov/

### CAVC Process
1. File notice of appeal within 120 days
2. Open brief on merits filed
3. VA files response brief
4. Oral argument (optional)
5. Court issues decision
6. Can appeal further to Federal Circuit

## Legal Aid & Pro Bono

### Legal Aid Organizations
- Some areas have free legal services for veterans
- Check https://www.lawhelp.org/
- State legal aid societies

### Law School Clinics
- Some law schools help veterans free
- Contact nearby law school
- Student supervised by attorney

## When You Need Legal Help

**File Simple Appeal Yourself**:
- HLR (Higher-Level Review)
- Evidence seems clear
- Straightforward issue

**Consider VSO Help**:
- Supplemental claim
- More complex situation
- Need guidance organizing evidence

**Consider Attorney**:
- Board appeal
- Complex legal arguments needed
- Lost multiple times

**Definitely Need Attorney**:
- CAVC (court) appeal
- Federal Circuit appeal
- Complex precedent issues

## Questions to Ask Before Hiring Attorney

1. What is your experience with VA claims?
2. How many cases have you won?
3. What is your fee structure?
4. Can you represent me at all appeal levels?
5. Will you communicate regularly?
6. What are the likely outcomes?
7. What is timeline?

## Your Rights

### Representation Rights
- Right to free VSO representation
- Right to hire private attorney
- Right to access your VA file
- Right to submit evidence
- Right to appeal
- Right to hearing

### File Access
- Get copy of C-file (claims file)
- Request through VA.gov
- Include all evidence VA has
- Help understand what VA is basing decision on

## Legal Costs and Fee Limits

### VSO Representation
- **Cost**: Free
- **Accreditation**: By VA
- **Authority**: 38 USC 5904

### Attorney Representation
- **Fee Cap**: Lower of:
  - 20% of retroactive backpay, OR
  - $6,000 (may increase annually)
- **Veterans Assistance**: Fees may be waived
- **Agreement**: Must be in writing

### Filing Fees
- VSO: Free
- CAVC appeal: Small filing fee
- Court processes: Various fees
