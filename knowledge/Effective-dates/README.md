# Effective Dates — When You Get Paid (And Retroective Money)

**The effective date is when your VA benefit officially starts.**

It determines:
- When your first payment arrives
- How much retroactive (back) pay you're owed
- When dependents' payments begin
- When rating changes take effect

**Getting the effective date wrong can cost you thousands in lost backpay.**

---

## Three Types of Effective Dates

| Claim Type | Effective Date | Backpay Possible? | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Original** | Date claim received (or earlier if evidence supports) | Up to 12 months before filing | File Sept 2025, approved Jan 2026 → May get backdated to Sept 2024 |
| **Increase** | Date you filed the increase | Usually NO | Filed increase Feb 2026 → effective Feb 2026, not back to original rating date |
| **Reopened** | Date of new application | NO | Filed again after denial → starts fresh, no retroactivity to original filing |

---

## Real Money Impact

**Lost by Wrong Dates = Hundreds to Tens of Thousands**

Example: 50% rating at $1,075/month  
- Effective Sept 2025 BUT should've been Sept 2024 = **$12,900 lost**
- Effective Jan 2026 BUT should've been Jan 2025 = **$10,750 lost**

**Lesson**: Effective date matters enormously. Fight for correct date!

---

## How to Maximize Your Effective Date

1. **File early** — Don't wait if you have diagnosis
2. **Get medical evidence before filing** — Proves earlier condition existence
3. **Appeal if dates wrong** — Even after approval, can request earlier date
4. **For increases** — File immediately when condition worsens (effective date = filing date)

---

## Original Claim Example

```
Day 1: Diagnosed with PTSD (October 2024)
Month 1: File VA claim (February 2025)
Month 9: VA approves (November 2025)

Best Case (if diagnosis documented before filing):
- Effective date: October 2024
- Backpay: Oct 2024 - Nov 2025 = 14 months
- At 20% ($343/month) = $4,802 backpay

Worst Case (if effective = filing date):
- Effective date: February 2025
- Backpay: Feb 2025 - Nov 2025 = 10 months
- At 20% ($343/month) = $3,430 backpay

Difference: **$1,372 lost** by delayed filing
```

## Quick Rules

| Situation | Effective Date | Backpay |
|-----------|---|---|
| Original claim, quick filing | Often claim date + 1 year prior if evidence | Usually 3-6 months |
| Original claim, delayed filing | Claim date (or 1 year before if evidence) | Whatever the date allows |
| Increase while already rated | Date you filed increase | Not before original rating |
| Reopened after denial | Date of new opening | Not to original denial |
| Adding spouse/dependents | When you notify VA | From notification to approval |

---

## Your Path to Maximum Backpay

1. **Get diagnosed**  
2. **File immediately** (don't wait years)
3. **Include ALL medical evidence** in claim
4. **Request early effective date** if evidence supports
5. **Read [Effective Dates Guide](effective-dates_guide.md)** to understand rules
6. **Appeal if date too late** with medical evidence of earlier condition
