# Glossary - VA Disability Terms

Quick reference for common VA disability terminology.

## A

**AMI** - American Medical Informatics
**Appeal** - Disagreeing with a VA decision using HLR, Supplemental Claim, or Board Appeal
**A&A** - Aid and Attendance; additional compensation for need of daily care

## B

**Board Appeal** - Formal appeal with hearing option before Veterans Law Judge
**Buddy Statement** - Letter from fellow service member confirming your service and condition
**Burn Pits** - Open burn areas in Iraq/Afghanistan; presumptive exposure

## C

**C&P Exam** - Compensation & Pension examination by VA medical professional
**CAVC** - Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims; highest level appeal
**CFR** - Code of Federal Regulations; contains VA disability rating schedules
**Combined Rating** - How VA combines multiple disability ratings using special formula
**COL/COLA** - Cost of Living Adjustment; annual increase to disability payments

## D

**DBQ** - Disability Benefits Questionnaire; standardized medical form
**DEERS** - Defense Enrollment Eligibility Reporting System; dependent registration
**DIC** - Dependency and Indemnity Compensation; benefits for survivors
**Direct Service Connection** - Condition caused directly by military service
**DVOP** - Disabled Veterans Outreach Program

## E

**Effective Date** - When benefits officially begin
**EBenefits** - VA's online benefits portal (now VA.gov)

## H

**HLR** - Higher-Level Review; fast appeal option (4-6 months)
**HISA** - Home Improvement and Structural Alteration grant

## I

**IU** - Individual Unemployable; unable to work due to disabilities

## L

**Lay Evidence** - Statement from non-medical person (buddy, family)
**LRAP** - Legal Representative Assistance Program

## M

**MST** - Military Sexual Trauma; sexual assault or harassment during service

## N

**Nexus** - Connection or link between service and disability
**Nexus Letter** - Doctor's opinion explaining how service caused condition

## O

**OSR** - Official Service Record

## P

**PACT Act** - Prevent All Cigarette Trafficking Act; covers burn pit exposure
**P&T** - Permanent and Total; 100% rating with no future reviews
**Prepon** - Presumptive overseas posting notice
**Presumptive** - VA presumes condition related to service without requiring proof

## R

**Rating Decision** - VA's official determination of disability rating
**Rating Schedule** - VA's standardized guidelines for assigning disability percentages
**Reopened Claim** - Previously denied claim submitted again with new evidence
**RFC** - Residual Functional Capacity; what you can still do despite disability
**RO** - Regional Office; VA office processing claims

## S

**SAH** - Specially Adapted Housing; grant for adapted home
**Secondary Service Connection** - One condition causes another service-connected condition
**Service Connection** - VA's determination that condition relates to military service
**SHA** - Special Housing Adaptation grant
**SMC** - Special Monthly Compensation; additional payment for severe disabilities
**Stressor** - Traumatic event that caused PTSD
**Supplemental Claim** - New evidence submission for existing claim

## T

**TDIU** - Total Disability Individual Unemployable; 50-60% rating = 100% pay
**TERA** - Temporary Early Retirement Authority; early military retirement program
**Traumatic Injury Protection (TSGLI)** - Insurance for servicemembers with traumatic injuries
**TRICARE** - Health insurance for active/retired military families

## U

**VA Form 21-526EZ** - Application for Disability Compensation
**VA Form 20-0996** - Higher-Level Review request
**VA Form 20-0995** - Supplemental Claim form
**VA Form 10182** - Notice of Disagreement/Board Appeal form
**VA Regional Office** - Local VA office processing claims
**VARLO** - VA Regional Loan Office

## V

**VA** - Veterans Affairs; federal agency serving veterans
**VAOIG** - VA Office of Inspector General
**VCAC** - Veterans Claims Assistance Commission
**VFW** - Veterans of Foreign Wars; veteran service organization
**VHI** - Veteran Health Identification card
**VR&E** - Vocational Rehabilitation and Employment; job training program
**VSO** - Veterans Service Officer; provides free assistance with claims

## W

**Wartime Service** - Military service during declared war period

## Common Rating Terms

**10%** - Minimal disability; slight impact on daily life
**20%** - Mild disability; noticeable symptoms
**30%** - Moderate disability; regular impact on activities
**40%** - Significant disability; notable limitations
**50%** - Major disability; can work with limitations
**60%** - Severe disability; significant work barriers
**70%** - Very severe; substantial limitations
**80%** - Critical disability; extreme limitations
**90%** - Severe disability with near-total limitations
**100%** - Complete disability; unable to work

## Filing Terms

**Original Claim** - First disability claim filed
**Claim for Increase** - Request to increase existing lower rating
**Intent to File** - Placeholder claim to protect effective date
**Pending** - Claim being reviewed by VA
**Under Development** - VA requesting additional information
**Decision Made** - VA has issued rating decision
**Appealed** - Decision under review using appeal process
