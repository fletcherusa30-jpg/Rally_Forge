## Glossary & Definitions

### Common Terms

- **Service Connection**: VA's determination that your disability is related to military service
- **Effective Date**: The date VA decides your benefits start (often retroactive)
- **Rating**: The disability percentage (0%, 10%, 20%... 100%)
- **Combined Rating**: How multiple disabilities are combined into one rating
- **Dependent**: Spouse, child, parent eligible for additional benefits
- **SMC**: Special Monthly Compensation for severe disabilities
- **DBQ**: Disability Benefits Questionnaire (medical form)
- **Preponderance**: "More likely than not" (over 50% chance)
- **Lay Statement**: Written statement from non-medical person with personal knowledge
- **Nexus**: Medical opinion connecting condition to service or to another condition
- **M21-1**: VA's internal adjudication manual
- **CAVC**: Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims (federal veterans court)

**See**: [Full Glossary](Glossary/)

## How to Use This Knowledge Base

### 1. **Choose Your Topic**
Use the Quick Navigation section at top, or browse by category below.

### 2. **Read the Guide**
Each guide follows this structure:
- **What This Means** — Plain English explanation
- **When It Applies** — Situations where this matters
- **How VA Decides** — The decision process
- **What Evidence Helps** — Documentation you should gather
- **Common Scenarios** — Real examples showing how rules apply
- **Related Regulations** — CFR citations and references

### 3. **Gather Evidence**
Use the "What Evidence Helps" sections to collect medical records, lay statements, and nexus letters.

### 4. **File or Appeal**
Apply rules to your specific situation. If you disagree with a VA decision, see [Appeals Guide](Appeals/).

### 5. **Get Help**
Contact a Veterans Service Officer or Accredited Representative for assistance (usually free).
