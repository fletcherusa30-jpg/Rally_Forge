# Backpay Explained

Backpay is retroactive disability compensation payment for the period between when your condition began and when VA approved your claim.

## How Backpay Works

### Calculation
- Monthly rate × number of months owed
- Based on effective date
- Includes dependents if applicable
- Tax-free payment

### Effective Dates
- Original claim: Up to 1 year before received
- Claim for increase: Date of claim
- Reopened claim: Date of application
- Effective date determines backpay

### Example
- Claim filed: March 2026
- Approved: September 2026
- Rating: 50% = \$1,092/month
- Backpay: 6 months × \$1,092 = \$6,552
- Paid in lump sum

## Payment Timing
- Lump sum payment after approval
- Usually within 30 days of notification
- Direct deposit to your bank account
- Can be substantial amount

## Tax Implications
- Backpay is tax-free (military disability)
- No 1099-R form issued
- Report as VA disability income

## Using Your Backpay
- No restrictions on how you spend it
- Many use for bills, debt, necessities
- Some invest or save for future
- Personal financial decision
