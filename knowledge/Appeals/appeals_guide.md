# Appeals Process

Guide to VA appeal options and procedures.

## Appeal Options

### 1. Higher-Level Review (HLR)
- **Timeline**: 1 year from VA decision date
- **Process**: Informal review (no hearing)
- **Decision Time**: Usually 4-6 months
- **Best For**: Clear errors, missing evidence only
- **Decision Ratings**: Usually same or lower

### 2. Supplemental Claim
- **Timeline**: Anytime (no deadline)
- **Process**: Submit new evidence
- **Decision Time**: Usually 3-4 months if submitted together
- **Best For**: New medical evidence available
- **Decision Options**: Any outcome possible

### 3. Board Appeal (VA.gov)
- **Timeline**: 1 year from VA decision date
- **Process**: Formal appeal with hearing option
- **Decision Time**: 4-12+ months
- **Best For**: Disagreement with VA reasoning
- **Hearing Options**: Video, Phone, or Written

## Appeal Flowchart

```
VA Issues Initial Decision
        ↓
  Disagree with Decision?
        ↓
   ┌────┴────┐
   ↓         ↓
 HLR    Supplemental   Board Appeal
(Fast)   (New Evidence) (Formal)
```

## HLR Process

1. Complete VA Form 20-0996
2. Select representative (optional)
3. Submit within 1 year
4. VA reviews for clear/obvious errors
5. Decision issued
6. If still disagree, can appeal again

## Supplemental Claim Process

1. Gather new evidence
2. Complete VA Form 20-0995
3. Submit anytime (no deadline)
4. VA considers all evidence
5. Decision issued
6. If disagree, can use HLR or Board

## Board Appeal Process

1. Complete VA Form 10182 (or submit online)
2. Choose hearing type:
   - Video hearing (most interactive)
   - Phone hearing
   - Written option (no hearing)
3. Await assignment to judge
4. Hearing or written review
5. Board decision issued
6. If still disagree, can appeal to CAVC

## CAVC Appeal

- **Timeline**: 120 days from Board decision
- **Court**: Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims
- **Process**: Legal appeal with attorney
- **Decision**: Binding legal precedent possible

## Key Documents Needed

- Decision letter explaining why you disagree
- Medical evidence supporting your claim
- DD-214 or service records
- VA rating decision
- Any communication with VA
- Witness statements (for HLR)

## Tips for Success

1. **Be Specific**: Explain exactly which parts you disagree with
2. **Provide Evidence**: New or corrected evidence strengthens case
3. **Get Help**: VSO or attorney can assist at no cost
4. **Meet Deadlines**: 1 year for HLR/Board from decision date
5. **Track Your Case**: Use VA.gov to check status
