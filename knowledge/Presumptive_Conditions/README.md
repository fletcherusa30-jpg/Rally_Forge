# Presumptive Conditions Database

This directory contains comprehensive data on VA presumptive service-connected conditions organized by exposure type.

## Coverage Areas

### Agent_Orange/
18 presumptive conditions from herbicide exposure (Vietnam, Korea DMZ, Thailand)

### Burn_Pits/
27 presumptive conditions from burn pit and airborne hazard exposure (PACT Act)

### Camp_Lejeune/
18 presumptive conditions from water contamination (1953-1987)

### Gulf_War_Illness/
14 chronic multisymptom illnesses and infectious diseases (1990-present)

### Radiation/
20 presumptive cancers from ionizing radiation exposure (Atomic Veterans)

## Data Format
Each subdirectory contains:
- `conditions.json` - Complete condition data with diagnostic codes, effective dates, requirements
- `README.md` - Overview and quick reference

## Total Coverage
- 97+ presumptive conditions across 5 exposure categories
- Diagnostic codes mapped for integration with rating schedule
- Evidence requirements documented
- Regulatory references (38 CFR, PACT Act, etc.)

## Integration
This data is used throughout Rally Forge for:
- Presumptive condition detection in scanners
- Automatic service connection determination
- Evidence requirement guidance
- Benefit eligibility screening
