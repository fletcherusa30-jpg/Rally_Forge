# Camp Lejeune Water Contamination Presumptive Conditions

This directory contains data on conditions presumed to be caused by contaminated water at Camp Lejeune.

## Data Files
- `conditions.json` - Complete list of 18 presumptive conditions (10 added by PACT Act)

## Coverage
- Service dates: August 1, 1953 - December 31, 1987
- Locations: Camp Lejeune, NC and MCAS New River, NC
- Requirement: 30 cumulative days of service

## Contaminants
- Trichloroethylene (TCE)
- Perchloroethylene (PCE)
- Benzene
- Vinyl Chloride

## Key Conditions
- 8 Cancers (bladder, kidney, liver, leukemia, lymphoma, multiple myeloma, lung, breast)
- Parkinson's Disease
- Kidney Disease (End-Stage Renal Disease)
- Female Infertility & Miscarriage
- Neurobehavioral Effects

See `conditions.json` for complete details.
