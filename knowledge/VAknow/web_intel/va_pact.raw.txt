What does it mean to have a presumptive condition for toxic exposure? To get a VA disability rating, your disability must connect to your military service. For many health conditions, you need to prove that your service caused your condition.&#xA0; But for some conditions, we automatically assume (or &#x201C;presume&#x201D;) that your service caused your condition. We call these &#x201C;presumptive conditions.&#x201D; We consider a condition presumptive when it&apos;s established by law or regulation. If you have a presumptive condition, you don&#x2019;t need to prove that your service caused the condition. You only need to meet the service requirements for the presumption.
