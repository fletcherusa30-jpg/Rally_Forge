# Survivor Benefits

Guide to benefits for survivors of deceased veterans and service members.

## Types of Survivor Benefits

### DIC (Dependency and Indemnity Compensation)
- **Purpose**: Monthly income for survivors
- **Eligibility**: Death from service-connected condition OR on-duty death
- **Recipient**: Surviving spouse, children, parents
- **Amount**: 2026 rates $1,502-$3,737/month for spouse + children

### Death Pension
- **Purpose**: Monthly income for low-income surviving spouses
- **Eligibility**: Wartime service; widow/widower needs care or age 57+
- **Amount**: 2026 rates up to $1,300/month (spouse alone)

### Burial Benefits
- **Burial**: Free national cemetery burial
- **Headstone**: Free marble headstone or marker
- **Flag**: American flag for casket or burial
- **Allowance**: Up to $2,000 burial allowance (if not in national cemetery)

## Who Qualifies

### Surviving Spouse
- Legally married at time of death
- Age 57+ OR caring for child under 16
- Remarriage may affect benefits

### Children
- Unmarried and under 18 (or 23 if in school)
- Disabled child regardless of age
- Dependent status must be established (DEERS)

### Parents
- If veteran was sole survivor
- Age 60+ (for DIC)
- Dependent on veteran at time of death

## Filing for Benefits

### Required Documents
- Death Certificate
- DD-214 or service records
- Marriage certificate (spouse)
- Birth certificates (children)
- Proof of enrollment if child in school

### Where to File
- **Online**: VA.gov
- **By Mail**: VA Regional Office
- **In Person**: Local VA Office
- **By Phone**: 1-800-827-1000

### Processing Time
- Usually 3-6 months
- Can take longer if documents missing
- Backpay to date of death

## Special Situations

### Service-Connected Death
- Clearly eligible for DIC
- No age restrictions on dependents
- Higher benefit amounts
- Priority processing

### On-Duty Death
- Death while on active duty
- Eligible for DIC regardless of service-connection
- May receive SBP instead
- Can eventually get DIC

### Non-Service Connected Death
- Can receive Death Pension (low-income)
- Strictures are tighter
- Income and asset limits apply
- Age/care requirements

## Burial and Memorial

### National Cemetery Burial
- Free burial in national cemetery
- Includes grave space, opening/closing
- Available to all eligible veterans
- Can reserve in advance

### Pre-Need Burial
- Determine eligibility in advance
- Help with planning
- Reserve cemetery space
- No cost

### Burial Allowance
- Up to $2,000 if buried privately
- Reimburses actual costs
- Must submit receipts
- Not available if national cemetery

### Presidential Greeting
- U.S. President sends greeting at milestone ages
- 80th, 85th, 90th, 95th, 100th
- Request through VA

## Special Cases

### Remarriage
- Spouse benefits stop if remarry (typically)
- Some exceptions based on age
- Death Pension has different rules
- Consult VA before remarrying

### Disabled Child
- Continues receiving benefits
- Regardless of age
- If eligible at age of death
- Must maintain disabled status

### Parent as Dependent
- Rare to qualify
- Usually if only child who supported parent
- Parent must be age 60+
- And dependent on veteran

## Application Documents Checklist

- [ ] Death Certificate

## Common Questions

**Q: When do benefits start?**
A: Effective date of veteran's death; retroactive

**Q: Can I work while receiving survivor benefits?**
A: Yes; no income limits for DIC (but limits apply to Death Pension)

**Q: Can I remarry and keep benefits?**
A: Varies by benefit type and age; consult VA

**Q: What if there are two dependents?**
A: Benefits divided among eligible survivors
