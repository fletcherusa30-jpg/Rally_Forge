# VA Overpayments

Information about VA overpayments and what to do if you owe money.

## What Is an Overpayment?

An overpayment occurs when VA pays you more than you're entitled to:
- Payments after death
- Duplicate payments
- Incorrect rating applied
- Benefit eligibility ended but payments continued
- Dependent removed but payments continued

## Overpayment Notice

### What You'll Receive
- Letter explaining overpayment
- Amount owed
- How it was calculated
- Debt collection options
- Your appeal rights

### 30-Day Response Period
- You have 30 days to respond
- Must file formal written response
- Can request waiver
- Can request payment plan

## Waiver Options

### Permanent Duty Waivers
Can request waiver of overpayment if:
- Through no fault of your own (VA's mistake)
- Would create undue hardship
- Repayment would dampen adjustment
- Not living beyond means

### Temporary Waivers
- Request while appeal pending
- Stops debt collection temporarily
- Still owes if not waived permanently

## Repayment Options

### Full Repayment
- Pay entire amount at once
- Stops interest/collection action
- Clean credit standing restored

### Payment Plans
- Fixed monthly amount
- Typically 30-60 months
- Must be reasonable amount
- Can request modification if hardship

### Offset
- VA withholds future payments
- Applied to overpayment debt
- Can request different offset amount

## Overpayment Appeals

### Appeal Process
1. Respond within 30 days of notice
2. State reason for disagreement
3. Provide supporting evidence
4. File through VA Regional Office
5. VA reviews decision

### Grounds for Appeal
- Calculation error
- Shouldn't have been overpaid (eligible)
- Waiver qualifies (through no fault of own)
- Amount is incorrect

## Special Situations

### Overpayment at Death
- If veteran dies, estate responsible
- Family may need to repay
- Some exemptions possible
- Contact VA about waiver

### Overpayment During Appeal
- If appealing rating decision
- May owe money if appeal denied
- But can request waiver
- Payment plan available

### Combat-Related
- Some combat-related overpayments may be forgiven
- Special authority available
- Contact VA about options

## Hardship Considerations

**Undue Hardship** exists if:
- Living at or below poverty level
- Medical/dependent care costs
- Fixed income unable to reduce
- Other emergency circumstances
