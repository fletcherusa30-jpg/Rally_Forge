# Burn Pit & Fine Particulate Matter Presumptive Conditions

**Authority:** 38 CFR §3.320, §3.320a, §3.320b | Updated: 2/12/2026 | **PACT Act**

## Overview
The PACT Act established presumptive service connection for diseases related to exposure to fine particulate matter (including burn pits) for veterans who served in specified locations.

## Qualifying Service Locations

Veterans must have served in:

### Southwest Asia Theater of Operations (Persian Gulf War)
- Iraq
- Kuwait  
- Saudi Arabia
- Neutral zone between Iraq and Saudi Arabia
- Bahrain
- Qatar
- United Arab Emirates
- Oman
- Gulf of Aden
- Gulf of Oman
- Persian Gulf
- Arabian Sea
- Red Sea
- Airspace above these locations

### Additional Qualifying Locations (On or After September 19, 2001)
- **Afghanistan**
- **Syria**
- **Djibouti**
- **Uzbekistan**

### Extended Coverage (§3.320a/§3.320b - "Covered Veterans")
On or after **August 2, 1990:**
- Southwest Asia theater (as defined above)
- **Somalia**

On or after **September 11, 2001:**
- Afghanistan, Djibouti, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Yemen, Uzbekistan

## Presumptive Diseases - Chronic Respiratory Conditions

**Authority:** 38 CFR §3.320(a)(2)

The following **chronic diseases** will be service-connected if manifested to **any degree** (including non-compensable) at **any time** following separation from qualifying service:

1. **Asthma**
2. **Rhinitis**  
3. **Sinusitis** (to include rhinosinusitis)

## Presumptive Diseases - Rare Respiratory Cancers

**Authority:** 38 CFR §3.320(a)(3)

The following **rare cancers** will be service-connected if manifested to **any degree** (including non-compensable) at **any time** following separation from qualifying service:

### Larynx & Trachea Cancers
1. **Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Larynx**
2. **Squamous Cell Carcinoma of the Trachea**
3. **Adenocarcinoma of the Trachea**
4. **Salivary Gland-Type Tumors of the Trachea**

### Lung Cancers (Rare Types)
5. **Adenosquamous Carcinoma of the Lung**
6. **Large Cell Carcinoma of the Lung**
7. **Salivary Gland-Type Tumors of the Lung**
8. **Sarcomatoid Carcinoma of the Lung**
9. **Typical and Atypical Carcinoid of the Lung**

## Additional PACT Act Presumptive Conditions

### Bladder & Urinary Tract Cancers (38 CFR §3.320a)
**For "Covered Veterans" as defined above:**

1. **Urinary Bladder Cancer** (including over-lapping sites of the bladder)
2. **Ureter Cancer** (including the ureteric orifice and urachus)

### Blood & Bone Marrow Cancers (38 CFR §3.320b)
**For "Covered Veterans" as defined above:**

1. **Acute Leukemias**
2. **Chronic Leukemias**
3. **Multiple Myelomas** (including Monoclonal Gammopathy of Undetermined Significance - MGUS)
4. **Myelodysplastic Syndromes (MDS)**
5. **Myelofibrosis**

## Presumption of Exposure

A veteran who has qualifying service **shall be presumed** to have been exposed to fine particulate matter during such service, unless there is affirmative evidence to establish the veteran was **not** exposed.

## Time Limits for Manifestation

- **Chronic Respiratory Conditions:** Any time after separation (no time limit)
- **Rare Cancers:** Any time after separation (no time limit)  
- **Bladder/Ureter Cancers:** Any time after separation (no time limit)
- **Blood/Bone Marrow Cancers:** Any time after separation (no time limit)
- **Non-compensable ratings qualify** for presumptive service connection

## Exceptions (Service Connection Will NOT Be Presumed If:)

1. **Affirmative evidence** that the disease was not incurred during or aggravated by qualifying service; OR
2. **Affirmative evidence** that the disease was caused by a supervening condition or event between the veteran's departure from qualifying service and disease onset; OR
3. **Affirmative evidence** that the disease is the result of the veteran's own willful misconduct

## Evidence Requirements

### What Veterans Need to Show:
- Service in a qualifying location during the specified time period
- Diagnosis of one of the presumptive conditions
- **No need to prove actual exposure** to burn pits or fine particulate matter (presumption applies)

### What VA Must Consider:
- Service records showing duty location
- Medical diagnosis of qualifying condition
- Date of manifestation (any time after separation qualifies)

## PACT Act Enhancements

The PACT Act significantly expanded:
- **Geographic coverage** (added multiple countries)
- **Condition coverage** (added cancers and blood disorders)
- **Eliminated time limits** for manifestation
- **Lowered threshold** (non-compensable ratings qualify)

## Special Considerations

### Burn Pit Registry
Veterans who served in covered locations are encouraged to:
- Register with the VA Airborne Hazards and Open Burn Pit Registry
- Document exposure through deployment records
- Obtain VA medical examinations

### Secondary Conditions
Conditions that develop as a result of presumptive conditions (e.g., cardiovascular issues from chronic respiratory disease) may also be service-connected under 38 CFR §3.310 (secondary service connection).

---
*This information is based on 38 CFR §3.320, §3.320a, and §3.320b as of February 12, 2026. The PACT Act has significantly expanded these presumptions. For claims purposes, always refer to the current Code of Federal Regulations and consult with a VA-accredited representative.*
