# Agent Orange & Herbicide Agent Presumptive Conditions

**Authority:** 38 CFR §3.309(e) | Updated: 2/12/2026

## Overview
If a veteran was exposed to an herbicide agent during active military, naval, or air service, the following diseases shall be service-connected if the requirements of §3.307(a)(6) are met, provided the rebuttable presumption provisions of §3.307(d) are satisfied.

## Presumptive Service-Connected Diseases

### Blood & Immune System Disorders
- **AL Amyloidosis**
- **All Chronic B-cell Leukemias** (including hairy-cell leukemia and chronic lymphocytic leukemia)
- **Hodgkin's Disease**
- **Multiple Myeloma**
- **Non-Hodgkin's Lymphoma**

### Cardiovascular Diseases
- **Ischemic Heart Disease** (including but not limited to):
  - Acute, subacute, and old myocardial infarction
  - Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease including coronary artery disease (including coronary spasm)
  - Coronary bypass surgery
  - Stable, unstable and Prinzmetal's angina
  - **NOTE:** Does NOT include hypertension, peripheral manifestations of arteriosclerosis such as peripheral vascular disease or stroke

### Metabolic & Endocrine Disorders
- **Type 2 Diabetes** (also known as Type II diabetes mellitus or adult-onset diabetes)
- **Porphyria Cutanea Tarda**

### Neurological Disorders
- **Parkinson's Disease**
- **Early-onset Peripheral Neuropathy**

### Skin Conditions
- **Chloracne or Other Acneform Disease** consistent with chloracne

### Cancers

#### Prostate & Respiratory Cancers
- **Prostate Cancer**
- **Respiratory Cancers:**
  - Cancer of the lung
  - Cancer of the bronchus
  - Cancer of the larynx
  - Cancer of the trachea

#### Soft-Tissue Sarcomas
**Soft-Tissue Sarcoma** (other than osteosarcoma, chondrosarcoma, Kaposi's sarcoma, or mesothelioma)

Includes the following specific types:
- Adult fibrosarcoma
- Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans
- Malignant fibrous histiocytoma
- Liposarcoma
- Leiomyosarcoma
- Epithelioid leiomyosarcoma (malignant leiomyoblastoma)
- Rhabdomyosarcoma
- Ectomesenchymoma
- Angiosarcoma (hemangiosarcoma and lymphangiosarcoma)
- Proliferating (systemic) angioendotheliomatosis
- Malignant glomus tumor
- Malignant hemangiopericytoma
- Synovial sarcoma (malignant synovioma)
- Malignant giant cell tumor of tendon sheath
- Malignant schwannoma (including malignant schwannoma with rhabdomyoblastic differentiation, glandular and epithelioid malignant schwannomas)
- Malignant mesenchymoma
- Malignant granular cell tumor
- Alveolar soft part sarcoma
- Epithelioid sarcoma
- Clear cell sarcoma of tendons and aponeuroses
- Extraskeletal Ewing's sarcoma
- Congenital and infantile fibrosarcoma
- Malignant ganglioneuroma

## Service Connection Requirements

### Vietnam Veterans
Veterans who served in the Republic of Vietnam during the Vietnam era are presumed to have been exposed to herbicide agents (§3.307(a)(6)).

### Other Locations
Exposure to herbicide agents may be established through:
- Service in locations where herbicide agents were tested, stored, or used
- Documentation of specific exposure incidents
- Other evidence of herbicide agent exposure during military service

## Evidence Requirements
- No medical evidence of the disease during service is required
- Must meet manifestation requirements per §3.307(a)(6)
- Subject to rebuttable presumption provisions of §3.307(d)

## PACT Act Expansions
The PACT Act has expanded the locations and time periods for presumed herbicide exposure. Consult current VA guidance for the most up-to-date service locations.
