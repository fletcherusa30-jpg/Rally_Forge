# Disability Benefits Questionnaires (DBQs)

DBQs are standardized forms medical professionals use to evaluate your disability in a way VA understands.

## What Is a DBQ?
- Standardized medical questionnaire
- Specific to each disability type
- Doctor completes with specific questions
- VA uses to assign ratings
- More detailed than regular medical exam

## Types of DBQs
- Joint conditions (back, knee, shoulder, etc.)
- Mental health (PTSD, depression, anxiety)
- Neurological (brain injury, migraine)
- Respiratory (sleep apnea, asthma, COPD)
- Cardiovascular
- And many others

## Getting a DBQ Completed
1. Download DBQ for your condition
   - https://www.va.gov/disability/dbq/
2. Provide to your doctor
3. Ask doctor to complete all sections
4. Schedule office visit if needed
5. Collect completed DBQ
6. Submit with VA claim

## Why DBQs Matter
- Provides standardized information
- VA raters can quickly assign appropriate rating
- Ensures consistent evaluation
- Can strengthen your claim
- Better results than generic medical records

## Tips for DBQ Success
- Choose doctor familiar with VA process
- Provide medical records to doctor
- Make appointment specifically for DBQ
- Ask doctor to explain answers
- Ensure all sections are completed
- Keep copy for your records
- Submit with claim immediately

## Using Private Doctor DBQs
- More detailed than private medical visit
- VA gives significant weight to doctor's opinion
- Private DBQs often result in higher ratings
- Worth the office visit cost
