# Service Connection — VA's First Step

**Service connection is the foundation of all VA benefits.** Without it, you get nothing. With it, you unlock disability compensation, healthcare, education benefits, and more.

---

## Quick Start

**How service connection works:**

1. VA determines your condition is service-related
2. You get rated (10-100% or 0%)
3. You receive disability payments monthly
4. You access VA healthcare and other benefits

---

## Three Types of Service Connection

| Type | Means | Example | Easiest |
|------|-------|---------|---------|
| **Direct** | Service caused condition | IED injury worsened | Military records |
| **Presumptive** | VA presumes for this service | Agent Orange → cancer in Vietnam vet | Just service dates + diagnosis |
| **Secondary** | SC condition caused another | Back pain → depression | Doctor opinion |

---

## Most Important Evidence (Ranked)

1. **Military medical records** from service era
2. **Nexus letter** from doctor (explaining connection)
3. **Current medical diagnosis** from doctor
4. **Buddy statements** from fellow service members
5. **Service records** documenting the incident
6. **Your detailed account** of what happened
