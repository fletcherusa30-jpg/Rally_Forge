## Key Information

### What These Rates Mean
- **Monthly amounts** are paid directly to the veteran each month
- **Annual amounts** are calculated as Monthly × 12
- **Additional increments** apply based on dependent status and number of children
- **100% rating** may include additional payments for Special Monthly Compensation (SMC) depending on severity

### Effective Dates
- These rates became effective **January 1, 2026**
- Rates are based on Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) Part 3, Schedule of Ratings for Disabilities
- Rates increased approximately **4.87% average** from 2024 to 2026

### How Ratings Are Determined
- Combined ratings are calculated using **VASRD (VA Schedule of Ratings for Disabilities)**
- Multiple conditions are not simply added together
- A complex formula determines the final combined percentage
- Individual condition percentages can range from 0% to 100%

### Dependency Payment Tiers
- **Spouse:** Spouse must be legally married
- **Children:** Payments for biological, adopted, or stepchildren under age 18 (or 23 if in school)
- **Parents:** Payments only at 100% rating if dependent on veteran for financial support
- **Additional Children:** Each child after the first adds the "additional child" increment
