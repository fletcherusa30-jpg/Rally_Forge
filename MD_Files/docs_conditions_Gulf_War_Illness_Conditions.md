# Gulf War Illness & Undiagnosed Conditions

**Authority:** 38 CFR §3.317 | Updated: 2/12/2026

## Overview
VA provides compensation for disabilities due to undiagnosed illnesses and medically unexplained chronic multisymptom illnesses in Persian Gulf veterans who served in the Southwest Asia theater of operations.

## Who Qualifies as a "Persian Gulf Veteran"?

A veteran who served on **active military, naval, or air service** in the **Southwest Asia theater of operations** during the **Persian Gulf War**.

### Southwest Asia Theater of Operations Includes:
- Iraq
- Kuwait
- Saudi Arabia
- Neutral zone between Iraq and Saudi Arabia
- Bahrain
- Qatar
- United Arab Emirates
- Oman
- Gulf of Aden
- Gulf of Oman
- Persian Gulf
- Arabian Sea
- Red Sea
- **Airspace above these locations**

### Persian Gulf War Time Period:
**August 2, 1990 through date to be prescribed by Presidential proclamation or law** (ongoing as of 2/12/2026)

## Qualifying Chronic Disabilities

### Definition
A **qualifying chronic disability** means a chronic disability resulting from:

1. **An Undiagnosed Illness**
2. **A Medically Unexplained Chronic Multisymptom Illness** defined by a cluster of signs or symptoms, such as:
   - **Chronic Fatigue Syndrome**
   - **Fibromyalgia**
   - **Functional Gastrointestinal Disorders** (excluding structural gastrointestinal diseases)

### Manifestation Requirements
Disability must have:
- **Become manifest** either:
  - During active service in Southwest Asia theater; OR
  - To a degree of **10% or more** not later than **December 31, 2026**
- **Cannot be attributed** to any known clinical diagnosis by history, physical examination, and laboratory tests

### Chronicity Requirement
Disabilities are considered **chronic** if they have:
- Existed for **6 months or more**; OR
- Exhibited **intermittent episodes** of improvement and worsening over a 6-month period
- Measured from the earliest date signs/symptoms first became manifest

## Signs & Symptoms of Undiagnosed Illness

Manifestations may include (but are not limited to):

1. **Fatigue**
2. **Signs or Symptoms Involving Skin**
3. **Headache**
4. **Muscle Pain**
5. **Joint Pain**
6. **Neurological Signs or Symptoms**
7. **Neuropsychological Signs or Symptoms**
8. **Respiratory System Signs/Symptoms** (upper or lower)
9. **Sleep Disturbances**
10. **Gastrointestinal Signs or Symptoms**
11. **Cardiovascular Signs or Symptoms**
12. **Abnormal Weight Loss**
13. **Menstrual Disorders**

## Medically Unexplained Chronic Multisymptom Illness

### Definition
A **diagnosed illness** without conclusive pathophysiology or etiology, characterized by:
- **Overlapping symptoms and signs**
- **Fatigue**
- **Pain**
- **Disability out of proportion to physical findings**
- **Inconsistent demonstration of laboratory abnormalities**

### Specific Conditions Included

#### Chronic Fatigue Syndrome
- Persistent or relapsing fatigue
- Not alleviated by rest
- Results in substantial reduction in activity levels

#### Fibromyalgia  
- Widespread musculoskeletal pain
- Fatigue
- Sleep, memory, and mood issues

#### Functional Gastrointestinal Disorders
Characterized by **chronic or recurrent symptoms** unexplained by structural, endoscopic, laboratory, or other objective signs:

**Specific Types Include:**
- Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)
- Functional dyspepsia
- Functional vomiting
- Functional constipation
- Functional bloating
- Functional abdominal pain syndrome
- Functional dysphagia

**Common Symptoms:**
- Abdominal pain
- Substernal burning or pain
- Nausea/vomiting
- Altered bowel habits (diarrhea, constipation)
- Indigestion
- Bloating
- Postprandial fullness
- Painful or difficult swallowing

**Diagnosis Requirements:**
- Symptom onset at least **6 months** prior to diagnosis
- Presence of symptoms sufficient to diagnose specific disorder at least **3 months** prior to diagnosis

### What is NOT Considered Medically Unexplained
**Chronic multisymptom illnesses of partially understood etiology and pathophysiology** will NOT be considered medically unexplained, such as:
- Diabetes
- Multiple sclerosis
- Other conditions with established pathophysiology

## Presumptive Service Connection for Infectious Diseases

### Gulf War Presumptive Infectious Diseases

The following infectious diseases will be service-connected if they become manifest in a veteran with qualifying Gulf War service:

1. **Brucellosis**
2. **Campylobacter jejuni**
3. **Coxiella burnetii (Q fever)**
4. **Malaria**
5. **Mycobacterium tuberculosis**
6. **Nontyphoid Salmonella**
7. **Shigella**
8. **Visceral Leishmaniasis**
9. **West Nile Virus**

### Manifestation Time Limits

#### General Rule:
Must become manifest to a degree of **10% or more** within **1 year** from separation from qualifying service

#### Exceptions:
- **Malaria:** Within 1 year OR when incubation period (per medical treatises) commenced during service
- **Visceral Leishmaniasis:** **No time limit**
- **Tuberculosis:** **No time limit**

### Qualifying Service for Infectious Diseases:
- Service in Southwest Asia theater of operations (as defined above); OR
- Service **on or after September 19, 2001** in **Afghanistan**

## Long-Term Health Effects of Infectious Diseases

### Table of Associated Conditions

If a veteran has (or had) one of the presumptive infectious diseases AND develops a related condition listed below, **VA must request a medical opinion** as to whether it is "at least as likely as not" that the condition was caused by the infectious disease:

#### Brucellosis Associated Conditions:
- Arthritis, Spondylitis
- Cardiovascular, nervous, respiratory system infections
- Chronic meningitis/meningoencephalitis
- Deafness, Sensorineural hearing loss
- Demyelinating meningovascular syndromes
- Episcleritis, Multifocal choroiditis, Nummular keratitis, Papilledema, Optic neuritis, Uveitis
- Fatigue, inattention, amnesia, depression
- Guillain-Barré syndrome
- Hepatic abnormalities (including granulomatous hepatitis)
- Myelitis-radiculoneuritis
- Orchioepididymitis/genitourinary infections

#### Campylobacter jejuni:
- Guillain-Barré syndrome (within 2 months)
- Reactive Arthritis (within 3 months)
- Uveitis (within 1 month)

#### Coxiella burnetii (Q fever):
- Chronic hepatitis
- Endocarditis
- Osteomyelitis
- Post-Q-fever chronic fatigue syndrome
- Vascular infection

#### Malaria:
- Demyelinating polyneuropathy
- Guillain-Barré syndrome
- Hematologic manifestations (anemia, splenic rupture)
- Immune-complex glomerulonephritis
- Neurologic/neuropsychiatric disease
- Ophthalmologic manifestations (retinal hemorrhage/scarring)
- Renal disease (especially nephrotic syndrome)

#### Mycobacterium tuberculosis:
- Active tuberculosis
- Long-term adverse health outcomes from irreversible tissue damage

#### Nontyphoid Salmonella:
- Reactive Arthritis (within 3 months)

#### Shigella:
- Hemolytic-uremic syndrome (within 1 month)
- Reactive Arthritis (within 3 months)

#### Visceral Leishmaniasis:
- Delayed presentation of acute clinical syndrome
- Post-kala-azar dermal leishmaniasis (within 2 years)
- Reactivation in context of future immunosuppression

#### West Nile Virus:
- Variable physical, functional, or cognitive disability

## Evidence Requirements

### For Undiagnosed Illness/Chronic Multisymptom Illness:
- Service in Southwest Asia theater during Persian Gulf War
- **Objective indications** of chronic disability (signs perceptible to examining physician or other independently verifiable indicators)
- Disability cannot be attributed to known clinical diagnosis
- Manifestation during service or to 10%+ by December 31, 2026

### For Infectious Diseases:
- Qualifying service (Southwest Asia or Afghanistan after 9/19/2001)
- Diagnosis of one of the 9 listed infectious diseases
- Manifestation within required time frame (if applicable)

## Exceptions (Service Connection Will NOT Be Established If:)

1. **Affirmative evidence** disability was NOT incurred during active service in Southwest Asia
2. **Affirmative evidence** disability was caused by **supervening condition/event** after service
3. **Affirmative evidence** disability resulted from veteran's **own willful misconduct** or **abuse of alcohol or drugs**

## Rating & Compensation

### How Gulf War Illnesses Are Rated:
- Rated using evaluation criteria from **38 CFR Part 4** (Schedule for Rating Disabilities)
- Based on disease/injury with similar:
  - Functions affected
  - Anatomical localization
  - Symptomatology

### Legal Status:
Qualifying chronic disabilities are **considered service-connected for purposes of all laws of the United States**
