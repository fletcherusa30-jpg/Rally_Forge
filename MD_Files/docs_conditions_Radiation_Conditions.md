# Radiation Exposure Presumptive Conditions

**Authority:** 38 CFR §3.309(d) | Updated: 2/12/2026

## Overview
Diseases listed below shall be service-connected if they become manifest in a radiation-exposed veteran, provided the rebuttable presumption provisions of §3.307 are satisfied.

## Presumptive Service-Connected Diseases

### Blood & Bone Marrow Cancers
- **Leukemia** (other than chronic lymphocytic leukemia)
- **Multiple Myeloma**
- **Lymphomas** (except Hodgkin's disease)

### Solid Cancers

#### Head & Neck Cancers
- **Cancer of the Thyroid**
- **Cancer of the Pharynx**
- **Cancer of the Salivary Gland**
- **Cancer of the Brain**

#### Thoracic & Respiratory Cancers
- **Cancer of the Breast**
- **Cancer of the Esophagus**
- **Cancer of the Lung**
- **Bronchiolo-alveolar Carcinoma**

#### Gastrointestinal Cancers
- **Cancer of the Stomach**
- **Cancer of the Small Intestine**
- **Cancer of the Pancreas**
- **Cancer of the Colon**
- **Primary Liver Cancer** (except if cirrhosis or hepatitis B is indicated)
- **Cancer of the Bile Ducts**
- **Cancer of the Gall Bladder**

#### Genitourinary & Reproductive Cancers
- **Cancer of the Urinary Tract** (kidneys, renal pelves, ureters, urinary bladder, and urethra)
- **Cancer of the Ovary**

#### Musculoskeletal Cancers
- **Cancer of the Bone**

## Who Qualifies as a "Radiation-Exposed Veteran"?

### Definition (38 CFR §3.309(d)(3))
A radiation-exposed veteran means a veteran who, while serving on active duty or as a member of a reserve component during active duty for training or inactive duty training, participated in a **radiation-risk activity**.

### Radiation-Risk Activities Include:

#### 1. Nuclear Testing
- **Atmospheric Nuclear Tests:** Onsite participation in a test involving the atmospheric detonation of a nuclear device (includes underwater nuclear detonations)

#### 2. Occupation of Japan (Post-WWII)
- **Hiroshima or Nagasaki Occupation:** August 6, 1945 through July 1, 1946
- **POW in Japan:** Internment as a prisoner of war in Japan (or service immediately following such internment) during WWII with opportunity for exposure to ionizing radiation comparable to occupation forces

#### 3. Gaseous Diffusion Plants
Service of at least **250 days before February 1, 1992** on the grounds of:
- **Paducah, Kentucky** gaseous diffusion plant
- **Portsmouth, Ohio** gaseous diffusion plant  
- **K25 at Oak Ridge, Tennessee**

**Requirements:**
- Monitored through dosimetry badges for radiation exposure during each of the 250 days; OR
- Served in a position with exposures comparable to a job monitored through dosimetry badges
- **Note:** "Day" refers to all or any portion of a calendar day

#### 4. Amchitka Island, Alaska
Service **before January 1, 1974** on Amchitka Island, Alaska, if exposed to ionizing radiation during duty related to:
- **Long Shot** underground nuclear test
- **Milrow** underground nuclear test
- **Cannikin** underground nuclear test

#### 5. Department of Energy Special Exposure Cohort
Service in a capacity which, if performed as a DOE employee, would qualify for inclusion as a member of the **Special Exposure Cohort** under section 3621(14) of the Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act of 2000 (42 U.S.C. 7384l(14))

#### 6. Enewetak Atoll Cleanup
**Cleanup of Enewetak Atoll:** January 1, 1977 through December 31, 1980

#### 7. Palomares, Spain Incident
**Onsite participation** in response effort following B-52 bomber collision that released four thermonuclear weapons:
- **Location:** Vicinity of Palomares, Spain
- **Period:** January 17, 1966 through March 31, 1967

#### 8. Thule, Greenland Incident
**Onsite participation** in response effort following B-52 bomber crash/fire that released four thermonuclear weapons:
- **Location:** Vicinity of Thule Air Force Base, Greenland
- **Period:** January 21, 1968 through September 25, 1968

## Onsite Participation Defined

### During Official Operational Period:
- Presence at the test site; OR
- Performance of official military duties in connection with ships, aircraft, or other equipment used in direct support of the nuclear test

### Six Months After Operational Period:
- Presence at the test site or other test staging area to perform official military duties
- Duties in connection with completion of projects related to the nuclear test (including decontamination of equipment)

### Garrison/Maintenance Forces:
Service as a member of garrison or maintenance forces on Eniwetok during:
- June 21, 1951 through July 1, 1952
- August 7, 1956 through August 7, 1957

## Evidence Requirements
- **No medical evidence** of the disease during service is required
- Must become manifest in a radiation-exposed veteran as defined above
- Subject to rebuttable presumption provisions of §3.307

## PACT Act Considerations
The PACT Act may have expanded radiation exposure recognition. Consult current VA guidance for the most up-to-date qualifying service locations and activities.
