# C&P Exams (Compensation & Pension)

The Compensation & Pension (C&P) examination is how VA evaluates your disability for rating purposes.

## What Is a C&P Exam?
- Medical evaluation for VA disability claim
- Performed by VA-contracted medical professionals
- Usually 30-60 minutes
- Results guide your disability rating

## What to Expect
- Appointment notice from VA
- Location (VA hospital, private clinic, telehealth)
- Medical professional asks questions
- Physical exam performed if applicable
- Medical tests may be ordered

## How to Prepare
- Bring appointment notice and ID
- Bring medication list
- Bring medical records if you have them
- Arrive 15 minutes early
- Comfortable clothing for exams
- List of symptoms/impact on daily life

## During the Exam
- Be honest about your symptoms
- Describe worst-case scenario
- Don't exaggerate or minimize
- Ask clarifying questions if confused
- Take notes on what was examined

## After the Exam
- Results sent to VA raters
- Can request copy of exam report
- Review for accuracy
- File dispute if errors noted
- Exam results heavily weight rating

## Common Exam Types
- Physical exams (musculoskeletal, medical)
- Psychiatric exams (mental health)
- Neuropsych exams (TBI, cognitive)
- Hearing/vision exams
- Specialized exams for specific conditions

## Tips for Best Results
1. Be specific about symptoms
2. Describe impact on daily life
3. Provide all medical records
4. Be consistent in describing condition
5. Don't minimize your condition
6. Explain functional limitations clearly
