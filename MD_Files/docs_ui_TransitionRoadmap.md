# Transition Roadmap

## Purpose
Provide a guided journey through claims, exams, evidence, appeals, and transition.

## Actions
- Pull onboarding, claims, exams, and evidence data.
- Present a step-by-step roadmap with statuses.
- Show what’s done, what’s pending, and what’s next.
- Highlight key milestones and deadlines.

## Responsibilities
- Claims timeline
- Evidence checklist
- Exam preparation
- Appeals path
- Education & transition benefits
- Retirement path
