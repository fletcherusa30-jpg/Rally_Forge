# VA Claims Process Guide

Complete guide to filing and managing VA disability compensation claims.

## Claim Types

### 1. Original Claim
- First claim for disability compensation
- File VA Form 21-526EZ
- Can include multiple conditions
- Based on service connection

### 2. Claim for Increase
- Existing condition has worsened
- Use existing claim number
- Provide new medical evidence
- VA re-rates condition at higher percentage

### 3. Secondary Claim
- New condition related to service-connected condition
- Example: Depression secondary to PTSD
- Must establish nexus to primary condition
- Part of existing claim

### 4. Reopened Claim
- Previously denied claim
- New and material evidence available
- File VA Form 20-0995
- VA reconsiders entire claim

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Prepare Documents
- VA Form 21-526EZ (complete online or paper)
- DD-214 or other service records
- Medical evidence of disability
- Address list of all military service

### Step 2: Submit Claim
**Online (Recommended):**
- VA.gov account required
- Upload supporting documents
- Track status in real-time
- Fastest processing

**By Mail:**
- Send to VA Regional Office
- Include all documents
- Slower processing
- Harder to track status

### Step 3: VA Notifies You
- Claim received notification
- Development letters if needed
- Requests for additional information
- Respond within deadlines to avoid dismissal

### Step 4: VA Exam (C&P)
- VA schedules Compensation & Pension exam
- Medical professional evaluates condition
- Exam usually 30-60 minutes
- Results sent to VA raters

### Step 5: Rating Decision
- VA assigns disability percentage
- Effective date determined
- Payment amount calculated
- Appeal rights explained

### Step 6: Payment Begins
- Direct deposit to bank account
- First payment appears
- Monthly payment received
- Annual COLA adjustments

## Important Timelines

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Effective Date | Date claim submitted or 1 year before VA received it |
| Development | 60-90 days for VA to request information |
| C&P Exam | 1-3 months after claim submission |
| Decision | 3-6 months on average (can be much longer) |
| Appeal | 1 year from decision date |

## What VA Rates

VA rates disabilities using 10% increments (0%, 10%, 20%, ... 100%)

- 0%: No disability but service connection granted
- 10-40%: Mild to moderate disability
- 50-70%: Moderate to severe disability
- 80-90%: Severe disability
- 100%: Total disability

## Combined Ratings

If you have multiple conditions, VA combines ratings:
- Does NOT add percentages (50% + 50% ≠ 100%)
- Uses VA Combined Rating Table
- Example: 50% + 30% = 65% (not 80%)

## Getting Help

### Free Resources
- **VA VSO**: Veterans Service Officer (free help at VA)
- **State VSO**: State veterans agency
- **VFW/American Legion**: Accredited representatives
- **Area Agency on Aging**: For older veterans

### Paid Resources
- **VA Accredited Attorney**: Can represent you
- **Veterans Law Firm**: Specialize in VA claims

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not explaining nexus**: How condition relates to service
2. **Incomplete forms**: Missing information delays claim
3. **Outdated medical evidence**: Get current exam letters
4. **Wrong figures**: Double-check all dates and numbers
5. **Missing deadlines**: 1 year for appeals from decision

## Evidence Tips

### Strong Evidence
- Current medical diagnosis
- Medical treatment records
- Doctor statements about service connection
- Buddy statements (lay evidence)
- VA examination results

### Weak Evidence
- No medical documentation
- Only self-reported symptoms
- Vague medical records
- No connection to military service
