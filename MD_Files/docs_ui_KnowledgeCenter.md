# Knowledge Center

## Purpose
Provide a structured library of CFR rules, presumptives, PACT Act conditions, and evidence requirements.

## Actions
- Present knowledge modules in a searchable, structured UI.
- Provide plain-language explanations of complex rules.
- Link CFR criteria and presumptives to scanner and claims intelligence outputs.

## Responsibilities
- CFR rating criteria
- Presumptive conditions
- PACT Act expansions
- Evidence requirements
- Decision examples
