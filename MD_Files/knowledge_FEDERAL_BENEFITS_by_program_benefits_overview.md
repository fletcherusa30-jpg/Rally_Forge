# Benefits Overview

Quick reference guide to main VA disability benefits.

## Compensation Benefits

### Disability Compensation
- Tax-free monthly payment
- Based on disability rating (10%-100%)
- **Spouse increases payment** by $13-$208/month depending on rating
- **Each child increases payment** by $39-$144/month depending on rating
- Annual COLA adjustments
- Can file claim at any time
- **See**: Dependent Rates Guide for detailed breakdown

### Special Monthly Compensation (SMC)
- Additional payment for severe disabilities
- Levels K through T
- Loss of limbs, blindness, aid&attendance
- Can combine with regular rating
- Separate calculation from base rating

### Total Disability Individual Unemployable (TDIU)
- 50% or 60% rating = 100% payment
- Must be unable to work
- Higher income than normal rating
- Requires separate application
- Can lose if employment occurs

## Healthcare Benefits

### VA Healthcare
- Comprehensive medical services
- Mental health and therapy
- Prescription medications
- Preventive care
- Dental (limited)
- Eligibility based on priority group

### TRICARE
- Health insurance for active/retired military
- Separate from VA benefits
- Can use both VA and TRICARE
- Copays apply to TRICARE

### CHAMPVA
- For family of veterans rated 100% P&T
- Or family of deceased from service-connected condition
- Covers medical and pharmacy
- Cost-sharing applies

## Education Benefits

### Post-9/11 GI Bill (Chapter 33)
- Full tuition at public university
- Monthly housing allowance
- Book stipend
- Transferable to dependents
- Up to 36 months of benefits

### Montgomery GI Bill (Chapter 30)
- Monthly stipend for education
- Lower benefits than Post-9/11
- For older cohort of veterans
- Can be transferred in some cases

### VR&E (Chapter 31)
- Vocational rehabilitation
- For 10%+ disabled veterans
- Job training and placement
- Education support
- Subsistence allowance while training

## Housing Benefits

### VA Home Loan
- Zero down payment
- Competitive interest rates
- No PMI (mortgage insurance)
- Available multiple times
- Up to $800,000+ loan amounts

### Adapted Housing Grants
- Grants to modify home for severe disabilities
- SAH (Specially Adapted Housing)
- SHA (Special Housing Adaptation)
- HISA (Home Improvement/Structural Alteration)

### Homeless Assistance
- Transitional housing
- HUD-VASH program
- Rapid re-housing
- Supportive services

## Dependent Benefits

### DEERS (Defense Enrollment Eligibility Reporting System)
- Register family as dependents
- Affects healthcare, education benefits
- Increases disability compensation

### Dependent Education (Chapter 35)
- For qualifying dependents
- Transferable GI Bill benefits
- Dependent education assistance
- Age and service restrictions apply

### Dependent Survivor Benefits (DIC)
- For family of deceased service member
- Monthly payment to spouse/children
- Non-service-connected death possible
- Based on death circumstances

## Survivor Benefits

### Death Compensation (DIC)
- Monthly to surviving spouse, children, parents
- Amount varies by relationship
- Service-connected death OR on-duty death
- Continues for life (spouse)
- Children until age 23 if in school

### Survivor Pension
- For low-income surviving spouses
- Wartime service requirement
- Age or indefinite care requirement
- Asset/income limits apply

### Burial Benefits
- National cemetery burial
- Headstone/marker
- Flag
- Burial allowance
- Presidential greeting (milestone)

## Employment Services

### Veterans Employment Services
- Job search assistance
- Resume review
- Employer matching
- Skills translation
- Computer access

### Disabled Veterans Outreach Program (DVOP)
- Intensive job development
- Training support
- Employer relations
- Priority for 30%+ disabled

### Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E)
- Formal vocational rehabilitation
- Job training and placement
- Education support
- Subsistence allowance while training

## Benefits Summary Table

| Benefit | Eligibility | Timeframe | Application |
|---------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| Disability Comp | Service-connected disability | Anytime | VA Form 21-526EZ |
| Healthcare | Honorable discharge | Anytime | VA.gov or clinic |
| Post-9/11 GI Bill | 90+ days post-9/11 active duty | Within use period | VA.gov or school |
| VA Home Loan | Honorable discharge, credit | Anytime | Lender + VA |
| VR&E | 10%+ disabled within 12 years | 12 years from separation | VA Form 28-1900 |
| DIC | Death service-connected/on duty | Anytime after death | Survivor files claim |

## How to Apply

### Online
- VA.gov account
- Upload documents
- Track status
- Fastest method

### By Mail
- Print forms
- Gather documents
- Send to VA Regional Office
- Slower processing

### In Person
- VA Regional Office
- Veterans Service Officer assistance
- Direct filing
- Interview possible
