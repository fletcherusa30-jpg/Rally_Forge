# Disability Ratings — What Percentage You Get

**Your rating percentage determines how much disability payment you receive monthly.**

- 0% = $0/month
- 20% = $343/month
- 50% = $1,075/month
- 100% = $3,738/month

**More evidence + higher rating = more money per month for the rest of your life.**

---

## How Ratings Work

**Step 1**: Service connection must be established (see [Service Connection section](../Service-connection/))

**Step 2**: You take a C&P exam (compensation & pension medical evaluation) performed by a doctor

**Step 3**: VA rater reviews your medical evidence and assigns a percentage

**Step 4**: You receive a decision letter with your rating and monthly payment amount

**Timeline**: Usually 3–6 months from C&P exam to decision

---

## What VA Looks At

- **Severity of symptoms** — How bad are they?
- **Functional limitations** — What can't you do?
- **Treatment needed** — What medications/care required?
- **Evidence quality** — Recent medical records (stronger) vs. vague complaints (weaker)

## Special Rating Situations

### SMC (Special Monthly Compensation)
Extra money for severe disabilities like loss of limbs, blindness, or needing a caregiver. Can pay $2,000–$7,900/month extra.

### TDIU / Individual Unemployable
Get 100% payment ($3,738/month) even if your rating is only 50-60%, if medically unable to work.

### P&T (Permanent & Total)
100% rating that won't be re-examined. Lifetime benefits.
