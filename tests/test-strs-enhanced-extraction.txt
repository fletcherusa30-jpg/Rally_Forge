Page 1

Medical Examination Report
Date: January 15, 2020

Patient presents with chronic back pain affecting lumbar region. Reports pain began during military service in 2018 after training accident. Pain is persistent and limits daily activities.

Assessment:
- Back pain, chronic
- Lumbar strain

Treatment:
- Physical therapy recommended
- Ibuprofen 800mg prescribed

Page 2

Follow-up Visit
Date: March 22, 2020

Patient returns with ongoing back pain. States pain has worsened and now radiates to left leg. Also reports new onset knee pain following same incident.

Assessment:  
- Back pain, persistent (chronic condition)
- Knee pain, patellofemoral syndrome
- Radiculopathy, left lower extremity

Medications:
- Ibuprofen continued
- Gabapentin 300mg added for neuropathic pain

Page 3

Follow-up Visit
Date: July 10, 2020

Back pain continues. Patient reports difficulty performing duties. Knee pain also persists. Line of duty event documented in service records from 2018 training accident.

Assessment:
- Chronic lumbar strain, ongoing
- Knee joint degeneration
- Duty limitation recommended: no heavy lifting

Page 4

Follow-up Visit  
Date: November 5, 2020

Patient presents with depression symptoms. Reports difficulty sleeping, anxiety, and loss of interest in activities. States symptoms began after 2018 injury and have worsened over time.

Assessment:
- Depression, major
- Anxiety disorder
- Insomnia

Medications:
- Sertraline 50mg prescribed for depression
- Hydroxyzine 25mg for anxiety

Nexus: Depression appears secondary to chronic pain condition from in-service injury.
