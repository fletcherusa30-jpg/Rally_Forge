VA RATING DECISION

DECISION

Service connection for anxiety disorder is denied. Service connection for
anxiety disorder is denied because the medical evidence of record fails to show
that this condition began in military service and the applicant has not met the
burden of proof needed to establish service connection under 38 CFR 3.303.

Service connection for PTSD is denied. Service connection for PTSD is
denied because the stressor event claimed by the applicant is not supported by
the service treatment records or other credible evidence. Military records do
not document the claimed incident, and therefore a diagnosis of PTSD cannot be
service-connected.

Service connection for back pain is granted with an evaluation of 20 percent.

REASON FOR DECISION

The applicant claimed service connection for anxiety, PTSD, and back pain.

Service connection for anxiety disorder: After careful review of the
evidence, we must deny this claim because the medical evidence does not
establish that the claimed anxiety disorder began in military service. While
the applicant was diagnosed with an anxiety condition, the evidence does not
show that military service was the cause. No incident reports from the applicant's
service period document the triggering event for the alleged anxiety condition.
The service treatment records show no notation of psychological distress. Recent
examinations have not established a service connection. Based on our review of
all evidence, we deny this claim.

Service connection for PTSD: The applicant claims that PTSD resulted from
a specific stressor event during military service. However, our investigation
found no record of the claimed event. The applicant was unable to provide
sufficient detail about the incident to locate corroborating evidence. Without
verification of the stressor event, we cannot establish that the diagnosed PTSD
is service-connected. Therefore, this claim is also denied under 38 CFR 3.303(c).

REFERENCES

38 CFR 3.303 - Presumptive conditions
38 CFR 4.40 - Functional loss due to pain
