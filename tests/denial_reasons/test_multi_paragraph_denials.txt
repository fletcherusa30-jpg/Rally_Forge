VA RATING DECISION - COMPLEX MULTI-PARAGRAPH DENIALS

DECISION SUMMARY

Service connection for erectile dysfunction is denied.
Service connection for tinnitus is granted at 10 percent.
Service connection for sinusitis is denied.

DETAILED REASON FOR DECISION

Condition 1: Erectile dysfunction

Service connection for erectile dysfunction is denied because the applicant
has not established that the condition began in military service or that it was
caused by a service-connected disability. The applicant claims that erectile
dysfunction resulted from back surgery while on active duty. However, the
service treatment records do not document any back injury during the claimed
period, and there is no evidence that military service caused the current
condition. The applicant's post-service medical records show the diagnosis was
made several years after discharge, and there is no consistent documentation
linking this to military service. The applicant was not examined for erectile
dysfunction during the service-connected disability evaluation for lumbar spine
condition. Without clear evidence of a causal relationship between military
service and the erectile dysfunction, we cannot grant service connection. The
evidence does not meet the requirements set forth in 38 CFR 3.303.

Condition 2: Acute sinusitis

Service connection for sinusitis is denied because there is insufficient
evidence that the condition is related to military service or any service-
connected disability. While the applicant claims the sinusitis resulted from a
sinus injury sustained during combat operations, the service treatment records
do not document any significant sinus trauma or treatment during the claimed
period. Post-service medical evidence shows that the applicant has a history of
seasonal allergies and upper respiratory infections, which are more consistent
with environmental causes than military-related injury. The applicant was seen
by an ENT specialist in 2015 who found no evidence of a residual service-
connected condition. Furthermore, sinusitis is a common condition that frequently
occurs without any service-related cause. The evidence does not establish a
nexus between military service and the claimed sinusitis condition. Therefore,
based on the requirements of 38 CFR 3.303, we must deny this claim.

PREVIOUS DECISIONS

Service connection for recurrent headaches is denied. Service connection for
recurrent headaches is denied because the evidence does not establish that the
recurrent headaches began during military service. While the applicant reports
experiencing headaches, the service treatment records show no documentation of
recurrent headaches or treatment during the service period. The post-service
medical evidence is limited and does not establish a clinical diagnosis of a
specific headache condition. The applicant's current neurological evaulation
did not support a diagnosis of migraine or other specific headache disorder
resulting from military service.
