DECISION

Service connection for fatigue is denied. Service connection for fatigue is denied because the medical evidence of record fails to show that this disability has been clinically diagnosed. The applicant submitted claims for various conditions, but the evidence does not support service connection.

Service connection for migraines is denied. Service connection for migraines is denied because the medical evidence of record fails to show that this disability has been clinically diagnosed and there is no etiological relationship established to military service.

Service connection for sleep apnea is granted with an evaluation of 50 percent effective November 27, 2017.

REASON FOR DECISION

25. Service connection for fatigue is denied.

Service connection may be granted for a disability which began in military service or was caused by some event or experience in service. Service connection for fatigue is denied because the medical evidence of record fails to show that this disability has been clinically diagnosed. Despite multiple examinations, no clinical diagnosis of fatigue as a distinct disability was established.

26. Service connection for migraines is denied.

Service connection may be granted for a disability which began in military service or was caused by some event or experience in service. Service connection for migraines is denied because the medical evidence of record fails to show that this disability has been clinically diagnosed. The claimed condition is not documented in the service treatment records or post-service medical evidence.

REFERENCES

Title 38 of the Code of Federal Regulations sets forth the regulations for rating disabilities.
