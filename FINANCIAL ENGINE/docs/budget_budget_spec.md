# Budget Planner Specification

## Data Model

### Income
- Salary
- VA
- Military Retirement
- Social Security
- Other

### Expenses
- Fixed: Mortgage, Insurance, Utilities, Phone, Internet
- Variable: Food, Gas, Entertainment, Misc
- Annual: Car Registration, Holidays, Property Tax

### Savings
- Emergency Fund
- Retirement
- Sinking Funds

### Debts
- Name
- Balance
- InterestRate
- MinPayment

## Outputs
- Monthly income total
- Monthly expense total
- Monthly surplus/deficit
- Category percentages
- 12-month projection
- Debt payoff timeline (snowball)
- Budget Health Score (0–100)
